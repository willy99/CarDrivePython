The Games

10 ways to challenge your mind

Each subgame trains a distinct cognitive skill. Together they form a complete daily brain workout.

🧠
Word Sequence

Words flash one by one. Memorize the exact sequence, then recall it in order. Early levels offer a word pool to pick from — later levels demand you type from pure memory.

12 levels Backwards mode Audio mode
👁️
Object Spotting

Study a grid of objects carefully. Look away while they rearrange. Find what disappeared — or in Intruder mode, spot the new object that appeared among the familiar ones.

Multiple packs Boss levels Intruder mode
➕
Math Drills

10 timed arithmetic tasks per session. Includes 6 mental math shortcuts not taught in school, a daily challenge shared globally, and an estimate mode (±10% accepted).

6 mental tricks Daily challenge Estimate mode
⚔️
Pairs Battle

Classic card-matching with a competitive edge. Challenge the AI at three difficulty levels, or face a real player online. Power-ups and ELO rating make every match matter.

AI opponent Online multiplayer ELO rating
💎
Diamond

A gesture pattern of connected dots is animated before you, line by line. Memorize the path — then trace it back from memory by dragging your finger across the dot grid.

19 levels 2×2 to 5×5 grid Drag to draw
🕰️
Long Memory

One curated item per day to memorize — from a beautiful word definition to a short story with embedded facts. Return 12+ hours later and write your recall. Spaced repetition schedules reviews automatically.

5 content tiers Spaced repetition Personal diary
🕵️
Colombo

Study a crime scene down to the last detail — the people, objects, colours and the clock. Then the scene vanishes and the detective grills you: who wore what, what time was it, what did the mirror reveal?

Crime scenes Detective twists Daily case
🔢
Cipher

Each digit 0–9 becomes a vivid shape. Link them into a wild little story, then rebuild the number from memory by dragging the shapes back in order. You learn a real mnemonic technique, not just drill.

Shape code Story building Drag to recall
🍽️
The Waiter

Customers arrive and place their orders. Memorize who wanted what, then serve every table correctly from memory — before they lose their patience.

Order recall Multiple tables Daily shift
🗣️
The Meeting

Watch a short business meeting unfold, then answer from memory — who promised what, which budget was named, whose birthday is coming up.

Read & recall Who said what Key details
