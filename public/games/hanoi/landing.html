How to play

Three towers, one rule

If you can count, you can start. Mastering it is another story.

1
Click a tower to lift its top disk.
2
Click another tower to drop the disk on top of its stack.
3
The one rule: a larger disk can never rest on a smaller one.
4
Your goal is to rebuild the entire stack on the last tower.
5
Beat the clock — later levels add a countdown timer, and your move count is always tracked.
💡 The clever bit
The fewest possible moves for n disks is 2ⁿ − 1. Three disks solve in 7 moves; eight disks take 255. Try to match the minimum — and the speed-run levels reward you for doing it fast.
