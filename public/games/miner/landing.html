How to play

Deployed and ready in 30 seconds

Everything you need before your first operation.

1
Tap any cell to drop your sapper on the field — the landing zone is always safe.
2
Read the numbers. Each revealed digit = how many mines touch that cell (diagonals count). Use logic to figure out where the mines are hiding.
3
Long-press / right-click a suspicious cell to plant a flag. Arrow keys or tap cleared ground to move your sapper without revealing.
4
Clear every safe cell to complete the operation. Use the artifacts from your backpack to get out of tight spots.
5
Pinch or scroll to zoom in/out. On PC you can also pan by dragging.
Features

More than just a grid

Everything that makes Бабах different from classic minesweeper.

🗺️
Topographic maps
Each level is a unique organic landscape — rivers, forests, mountains, islands, even coastlines. No two maps look alike.
🧍
Walking sapper
In Arcade mode your sapper physically walks the field. Tap revealed ground or use arrow keys to navigate without revealing.
🎒
Backpack system
Collect gear on the field, store it in your Stash and pick up to 2 artifacts before each new operation.
🌫
Fog of war
Fog levels hide everything beyond 5 cells. Night levels shrink visibility to 3. Explore carefully — or use a flashlight.
🌙
Night operations
Dark sky, moon, starfield. Only a tiny halo of light around your sapper. Use Flashlight or Spotlight to see further.
⏱
Timed missions
Some operations have a countdown. Clear the field before the clock hits zero — the timer turns red under 30 seconds.
🌉
Terrain elements
Rivers with bridges, lakes, seas, mountain ranges, tree clusters, and winding dirt roads — each changes how you navigate.
⭐
Rank progression
Clear more fields, climb the ranks: Recruit → Sapper → Sergeant → Lieutenant → Captain → Major → Colonel → General.
