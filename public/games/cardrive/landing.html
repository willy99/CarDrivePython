How to play

Behind the wheel in 30 seconds

Everything you need to know before your first drive.

1
Steer with the arrow keys — Up to accelerate, Down to brake and reverse, Left/Right to turn.
2
Reach the goal marker by following the road. In Taxi mode, pick up your passenger first, then drop them at the destination.
3
Drive clean — avoid crashes, obey the traffic and watch for speed cameras. Smooth, safe driving earns bonus points.
4
Mind your fuel — on fuel levels, top up at a gas station before the tank runs dry.
5
Press SPACE to advance to the next level, or R to retry the current one.

Features

Small game, big details

🌙
Night driving
Your headlights cut a cone of light through the dark — watch the edges of the road.
🌧️
Rain & weather
Wet roads change how the car grips. Adjust your speed before the corner.
⛽
Fuel management
On some levels the tank drains as you drive — refuel at stations or you'll stall.
📷
Speed cameras
Get caught speeding and you lose points. Ease off where the cameras watch.
💨
Drift bonus
Hold a clean slide through a turn and rack up extra style points.
🚦
Living traffic
AI cars, traffic lights and pedestrians make every street feel alive.
